** Color commentary ... the credit bureaus make it hard, on purpose.

   Security and Privacy of your data not a priority for the credit
   bureaus
*** They had to be forced to let you monitor your own data

    The credit bureaus collect data on you without your consent,
    largely for the purpose of selling it, again without your consent (or reimbursement.)
    They had to be forced by legislation to let you have free access to
    your own data:

    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AnnualCreditReport.com

*** Experian lost all it's data (YOUR data) 10 years ago

    Paranoid about sharing your social security number?  That pales in
    comparison to the 2015 breach of Experian:

    #+begin_quote
    ...During the time that ID theft service was in operation, customers [of
    the hacker's] service had access to more than 200 million consumer records.
    Experian didn’t detect [the] activity until it was notified by federal
    investigators...

    https://krebsonsecurity.com/2015/10/experian-breach-affects-15-million-consumers/

    #+end_quote


*** Experian has been negligent in responding to reported security issues for 9 years

    Nine years later, they were still leaking peoples data ...

    https://infosec.exchange/@briankrebs/111383547431806668

*** Freezing (vs locking) your credit report is made not obvious

    The credit bureaus are required to let you freeze your credit
    to make it harder for someone to open fraudulent credit in your
    name.  When you create an Experian account, your are asked to
    upgrade (pay for) your account, to sign up for their account "lock"
    service (which costs), etc.   I had to spend 10 minutes searching
    on the site for the menu that let's me freeze/verify I have frozen
    my account (You have to go to "Help Center -> Quick Actions ->
    Manage Freeze" ... of course that might change)

    And on it goes.

*** Similar motiviations, buggy user interfaces
    I could not see a way on the equifax site to simply save my whole
    report in a single PDF, so I went to AnnualCreditReport.com and was
    able to do (why did they make it hard to do on their site?).  When
    I got there and clicked "show me all 3 reports", one reported
    "technical trouble" generating the reoprt ("who could ever question
    'technical trouble' ?"), and when I got to the transunion report,
    it would NOT let me navigate back to the menu bar to hit the
    back-arrow without me inputting an email address and consenting to
    being sent marketing updates.  I gave them a bogus address.  There
    was still no way to natigate back or get to the next report.   They
    do not have incentive to make this site work well for consumers.

    Good luck.
